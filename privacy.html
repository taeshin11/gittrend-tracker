1. Introduction

GitTrend Tracker ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website located at gittrend-tracker.vercel.app (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of every developer and user who visits our platform. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, your rights regarding your data, and how to contact us with privacy concerns.

By using the Service, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please stop using the Service. We encourage you to review this policy periodically, as we may update it from time to time.

2. Information We Collect

GitTrend Tracker is a tool-first service. We do not require any registration, login, or personal information to use the Service. Information collection falls into the following categories:

2a. Anonymous Analytics Data

When you access GitTrend Tracker, our analytics system may automatically log the following anonymous technical data:

  • Timestamp of the visit
  • Page URL visited
  • Browser user agent string (e.g., "Firefox 121 on macOS")
  • Browser language and locale setting
  • Approximate timezone
  • Language filter and time range selections made during the session

This data is entirely anonymous. It cannot be used to identify any individual user. It is collected solely to help us understand how the Service is being used, monitor performance, and make improvements.

2b. Browser localStorage

To improve performance and user experience, GitTrend Tracker uses your browser's localStorage to store:

  • Cached API responses from GitHub (to reduce load times and API call frequency)
  • Your preferred interface language
  • Your most recently selected language filter and time range

This data stays entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser's site data or localStorage settings.

We do not collect: your name, email address, IP address in personally identifiable form, device identifiers, financial information, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).

3. How We Use the Data We Collect

The anonymous data we collect is used exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Monitoring aggregate visitor traffic to understand usage patterns
  • Identifying which language filters, time ranges, and features are most popular
  • Detecting technical errors, API failures, or performance degradation
  • Informing decisions about which languages to add support for in the future
  • Assessing whether the multi-language interface is reaching developers in different regions effectively

We do not use any collected data for advertising targeting, user profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Cookies and Third-Party Tracking Technologies

GitTrend Tracker itself does not set first-party cookies. We use browser localStorage (not cookies) for all first-party data storage. However, third-party advertising services we use may set cookies and employ other tracking technologies. We disclose these fully below:

Google AdSense

GitTrend Tracker displays advertisements served by Google AdSense, a service operated by Google LLC. Google uses cookies, web beacons, and related technologies to serve ads that may be personalized based on your browsing history and interests across websites that use Google's advertising services. You can review Google's data practices in their Privacy Policy and manage your personalized ad preferences at Google Ad Settings. You may also opt out of interest-based advertising from Google and other network members via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out tool.

Adsterra

We also use Adsterra as an advertising partner. Adsterra may use cookies and tracking pixels to deliver, measure, and optimize advertisements displayed on our Service. Their data collection and use practices are governed by the Adsterra Privacy Policy. You can manage Adsterra's cookie access through your browser's cookie controls or a browser extension designed for ad and tracker blocking.

You can control which cookies your browser accepts via your browser's privacy or cookie settings. Disabling third-party cookies may limit the relevance of advertisements shown but will not affect the core functionality of GitTrend Tracker.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

GitTrend Tracker integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its functionality:

  • GitHub REST API (GitHub Inc./Microsoft): Used to fetch public repository data. GitTrend Tracker sends API queries to GitHub but does not transmit any user information to GitHub. GitHub's own Privacy Statement governs their data practices.
  • Google AdSense (Google LLC): Serves advertisements. May receive technical session data for ad delivery.
  • Adsterra: Serves advertisements. May receive technical session data for ad delivery.
  • Google Sheets (via webhook): Anonymous, aggregate visit count data may be logged to a private Google Sheet for traffic monitoring purposes. No personally identifiable information is included.
  • Google Fonts: We load web fonts (Inter) from Google Fonts. Google may log font request metadata per their Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties. We do not share any user information with employers, recruiters, financial institutions, or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous visit analytics data, if logged, is retained for up to 12 months for trend analysis and then deleted. Browser localStorage data stored on your device persists until you manually clear your browser's site data or until the cache expiration times (30 minutes for daily data, 1 hour for weekly, 2 hours for monthly) are reached and the data is refreshed. We maintain no long-term database of personally identifiable user information, because we do not collect it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Introduction

GitTrend Tracker ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at gittrend-tracker.vercel.app.

Information We Collect

We do not require any user accounts or personal information to use our service. We may collect the following anonymous data for analytics purposes:

  • Page visit timestamps
  • Pages visited and filter selections (language, time range)
  • Browser user agent string
  • Browser language preference
  • Timezone

No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or stored.

Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to cache API responses for better performance and to store your language preference. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

  • GitHub API: We fetch data from GitHub's public API. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense for advertising. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Adsterra: We may use Adsterra for advertising. See Adsterra's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sheets: Anonymous visit data may be logged to Google Sheets for analytics.

Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies. Third-party ad services may use cookies as described above.

GDPR Compliance

As we do not collect personally identifiable information, GDPR data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure) do not typically apply. If you have concerns, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
